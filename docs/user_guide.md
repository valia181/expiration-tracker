# Welcome to Expiration Tracker!

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Expiration Tracker**, your personal assistant for keeping track of household supplies, food, medicines, cosmetics, and cleaning chemicals. This app helps you prevent spoilage, reduce waste, and ensure that nothing in your home goes expired unnoticed.

---

## 2. Main Dashboard (Item List)
When you open the app, you will see your main dashboard. All your tracked items are listed here in chronological order, with the items closest to expiration appearing right at the top.

Each item displays a color-coded status indicator so you can tell its freshness at a glance:
- **Red (Expired):** The expiration date has already passed. The item remains in your list until you use it or delete it.
- **Orange (Warning):** The expiration date is approaching soon within your configured alert window.
- **Green (Safe):** The item is fresh and far away from its expiration date.

---

## 3. Managing Items

### Adding a New Item
1. Tap the **Add Item** button (floating plus icon) on the main screen.
2. Enter the **Title** of the item (e.g., "Milk" or "Aspirin").
3. Select the **Expiration Date** using the date picker.
4. Choose a **Category** from the list: **Food**, **Medicine**, **Cosmetics**, or **Chemicals**.
5. Optionally, enter a **Storage Location** (e.g., "Refrigerator", "Pantry", or "Bathroom Cabinet").
6. Tap **Save** to add the item to your dashboard.

### Editing an Item
1. Tap on the item card you wish to update.
2. Modify the title, expiration date, category, or storage location as needed.
3. Tap **Save** to update your changes.

### Deleting an Item
1. When you have finished or discarded an item, tap the delete action on the item card.
2. Confirm the deletion when prompted, and the item will be permanently removed from your list.

---

## 4. Filtering and Searching

### Searching by Title
- Use the **Search Bar** at the top of the screen to quickly find items by typing their name. The list will instantly filter as you type.

### Filtering by Category
- Use the **Category Filter Chips** (All, Food, Medicine, Cosmetics, Chemicals) just below the search bar to show only items belonging to a specific category. Tap **All** to view everything at once.

---

## 5. Notifications
The app runs quiet background checks to make sure you never miss an expiring item:
- You will receive timely push notifications on your device when items enter the **Orange (Warning)** or **Red (Expired)** zones.
- Tapping a notification will alert you so you can take action before items go to waste.

---

## 6. Settings & Data Backup

### Customizing Alert Timings
1. Tap the **Settings** icon on the app screen.
2. Choose your preferred **Notification Lead Time** (how many days in advance you want to be warned: **1, 3, 7, or 14 days**).
3. Save your settings, and your item status alerts will update automatically.

### Backing Up and Restoring Your Data
- **Export Backup:** You can export all your tracked items and settings into a secure backup file saved to your device or cloud storage.
- **Import Backup:** If you switch devices or want to restore previous data, simply select your saved backup file to instantly restore your entire inventory.
